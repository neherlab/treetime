## Callouts

:::note
A note adds context that a reader may want to know.
:::

:::tip
A tip shows a shorter or better way to reach a result.
:::

:::warning
A warning names a result that can mislead a reader.
:::

:::danger
A danger notice names a step that loses data or gives wrong results.
:::

:::note[Custom title]
A callout can carry a title of its own.
:::
