## Line length

This long paragraph shows the line length and the wrapping of body text. The article column holds about seventy-two characters per line, a length that keeps the eye on the line while it moves from the end of one line to the start of the next. Words wrap at spaces, and the last line of a paragraph avoids a single short word where the browser supports it.
