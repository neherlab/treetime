## Display math

<a id="eq-jc69"></a>

$$
P_{ii}(t) = \tfrac{1}{4} + \tfrac{3}{4} e^{-4t/3}, \qquad P_{ij}(t) = \tfrac{1}{4} - \tfrac{1}{4} e^{-4t/3} \tag{1}
$$

where:

- $P_{ii}(t)$ is the probability that a site keeps its state over the branch length $t$
- $P_{ij}(t)$ is the probability that a site changes to one other given state, for $i \neq j$
- $t$ is the branch length in expected substitutions per site

Equation [(1)](#eq-jc69) gives the transition probabilities of the Jukes-Cantor model.

## Aligned derivation

<a id="eq-rtt"></a>

$$
\begin{aligned}
d_i &= \mu \, (t_i - t_0) + \varepsilon_i \\
\expect{d_i} &= \mu \, t_i - \mu \, t_0
\end{aligned} \tag{2}
$$

where:

- $d_i$ is the root-to-tip distance of tip $i$
- $t_i$ is the sampling date of tip $i$, and $t_0$ is the date of the root
- $\mu$ is the clock rate, and $\varepsilon_i$ is the residual of tip $i$

The slope of the regression in equation [(2)](#eq-rtt) estimates the clock rate.

## Matrix

<a id="eq-gtr"></a>

$$
Q = \begin{pmatrix}
\cdot & \pi_C r_{AC} & \pi_G r_{AG} & \pi_T r_{AT} \\
\pi_A r_{AC} & \cdot & \pi_G r_{CG} & \pi_T r_{CT} \\
\pi_A r_{AG} & \pi_C r_{CG} & \cdot & \pi_T r_{GT} \\
\pi_A r_{AT} & \pi_C r_{CT} & \pi_G r_{GT} & \cdot
\end{pmatrix} \tag{3}
$$

where:

- $Q$ is the rate matrix of the general time-reversible model, with each diagonal entry set so that its row sums to zero
- $\pi_A, \pi_C, \pi_G, \pi_T$ are the equilibrium frequencies of the states
- $r_{XY}$ is the exchangeability of the states $X$ and $Y$
