# Per-edge optimizer constants lack rationale, supported rationale, or validation

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Decision required.** No constant of the per-edge optimizer comes from v0. v0 has a different solver setup: scipy Brent on a bounded three-point bracket in $s = \sqrt{t}$ (outer points $\pm\sqrt{4}$, `MAX_BRANCH_LENGTH = 4`), a relative tolerance, and a soft penalty on the profile path. Every v1 value below is a v1 choice. Each constant needs a derivation, a measurement, or a decision to replace it with the v0 behavior or the behavior of an established tool as part of the matching solver contract. Recording an existing rationale changes no outputs. Changing a tolerance, bound, or fallback rule can change outputs and needs approval. A constant that is never reached on the measured runs (an iteration cap) can change without changing outputs.

## Problem

The constants of the per-edge branch-length optimizer carry no rationale in the code, the tests, or the KB. The history of v1 (`git log`) records a reason for some of them. The table traces each constant through the v1 history and compares it with v0 (`packages/legacy/treetime`) and established tools. ### Missing rationale

No reason is recorded for these constants or for the numeric value inside a recorded reason:

- `NEWTON_REL_TOL`, `NEWTON_MAX_ITER`, `BRENT_MAX_ITER`, the Brent tolerance (a library default), and the grid lower end $0.1 \cdot \text{one\_mutation}$
- The factor $1.5$ and the term $\text{one\_mutation}$ of the upper end
- The values $10^{-8}$ (`NEWTON_ABS_TOL`), $10^{-12}$ (bracket lower end), $0.01$ (indel floor), $100$ (grid points), and the step limit of one branch-length unit of the Newton clamp: the history records why each constant exists but not why it has this value. The coordinate change is not the unexplained part: the clamp $\delta_s \geq s - \sqrt{s^2 + 1}$ follows from limiting the increase of $t$ to one

### Unsupported rationale

A recorded or implied reason that the evidence does not support:

- **Bracket lower end `1e-12`**: `fa55c9d6` asks for a positive value below the grid lower bound and states no reason for positivity. argmin does not require it: it accepts a zero lower end and starts at an interior point. Zero may need exclusion where the objective is undefined at $t = 0$ (see [N-optimize-zero-boundary-handled-in-several-places.md](N-optimize-zero-boundary-handled-in-several-places.md))
- **`GRID_SEARCH_MIN_UPPER = 0.5` as "the biologically plausible range"**: the range excludes the global maximum $t \approx 6.37$ of the Dinh and Matsen K80 test surface for every start $t_0 \leq 2$ (a stress fixture, not real data; see [N-optimize-grid-search-upper-bound-capped.md](N-optimize-grid-search-upper-bound-capped.md))
- **IQ-TREE as a source for the grid lower end**: IQ-TREE sets its minimum branch length to $0.1 / \text{sites}$ only for alignments of $10^5$ sites or more that are not supertrees and when no positive minimum was supplied (`main/phyloanalysis.cpp`, revision `63c330d9`); otherwise it uses $10^{-6}$. This is a limit of the whole optimizer, so it does not justify a fallback grid cutoff
- **v0 as a source for the upper bound**: on the v0 path `MAX_BRANCH_LENGTH = 4` bounds the Brent bracket, so it is a hard limit of the search, not only a penalty scale. The v1 bound $\max(1.5 t + \text{one\_mutation}, 0.5)$ differs from it in both directions

### Missing validation

Accuracy and coverage of the recorded choices. Measurements (below) cannot prove universal grid coverage:

- Sufficiency of 100 log-spaced grid points: the grid is not refined, and the best point can be about 5% away from the maximum
- Sufficiency of the Brent absolute tolerance on long genomes, where $\text{one\_mutation}$ is below the tolerance
- Iteration budgets on datasets above 200 tips and on fixed non-unimodal models

## Evidence on current effects

From the measurements in [M-optimize-newton-returns-unverified-point.md](M-optimize-newton-returns-unverified-point.md#evidence) and [N-optimize-brent-ignores-termination-status.md](N-optimize-brent-ignores-termination-status.md#evidence) (`flu/h3n2/{20,200}`, `ebola/{20,100}`, `zika/86`, `rsv/a/100`):

- `BRENT_MAX_ITER = 50` was never reached (largest count 29)
- `NEWTON_MAX_ITER = 10` was reached on 2 of 9,544 Newton optimizations
- The grid lower bound $0.1 \cdot \text{one\_mutation}$ excluded the maximum on 4 Newton optimizations (`ebola/100`)
- The upper bound $\max(1.5 t + \text{one\_mutation}, 0.5)$ excludes the global maximum $t \approx 6.37$ of the Dinh and Matsen K80 test surface for every start $t_0 \leq 2$

## Proposed action

For each constant, record one of: a derivation, a sufficiency measurement, or an approved replacement (v0 behavior or an established tool's behavior). Use derivations for coordinate transformations and admissibility conditions, and measurements for practical accuracy and iteration budgets. Replace a constant with reference behavior only as part of the corresponding solver contract; copying an isolated value such as 4, 100, or 500 carries over none of the surrounding solver design. The carrier of the reason is a name, a type, a test, or the commit message, because the project bans explanatory comments in Rust source.
