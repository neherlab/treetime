# Per-edge optimizer constants have no stated reason

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Decision required.** No constant of the per-edge optimizer comes from v0: v0 has a different solver setup (scipy Brent on an unbounded $s = \sqrt{t}$ with a soft penalty), and every v1 value below is a v1 choice. Each constant needs either a derivation, a measurement, or a decision to replace it with the v0 behavior or the behavior of an established tool. Changing any of them changes optimizer outputs.

## Problem

The constants of the per-edge branch-length optimizer have no recorded reason in the code, the tests, or the KB. The table traces each one through the v1 history and compares it with v0 (`packages/legacy/treetime`) and established tools.

## Evidence on current effects

From the measurements in [M-optimize-newton-returns-unverified-point.md](M-optimize-newton-returns-unverified-point.md#evidence) and [N-optimize-brent-ignores-termination-status.md](N-optimize-brent-ignores-termination-status.md#evidence) (`flu/h3n2/{20,200}`, `ebola/{20,100}`, `zika/86`, `rsv/a/100`):

- `BRENT_MAX_ITER = 50` was never reached (largest count 29)
- `NEWTON_MAX_ITER = 10` was reached on 2 of 9,544 Newton optimizations
- The grid lower bound $0.1 \cdot \text{one\_mutation}$ excluded the maximum on 4 Newton optimizations (`ebola/100`)
- The upper bound $\max(1.5 t + \text{one\_mutation}, 0.5)$ excludes the global maximum $t \approx 6.37$ of the Dinh and Matsen K80 test surface for every start $t_0 \leq 2$

## Proposed action

For each constant, record one of: a derivation, a sufficiency measurement, or an approved replacement (v0 behavior or an established tool's behavior). The carrier of the reason is a name, a type, a test, or the commit message, because the project bans explanatory comments in Rust source.
